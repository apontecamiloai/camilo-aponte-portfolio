## Context

At a couple hundred articles, manually tagging content by product feature is tedious but manageable. At over two thousand, inconsistent or missing tags quietly break both human search and AI retrieval — a correctly written article that's tagged wrong is effectively invisible to whoever's looking for it.

## Approach

The tagger classifies knowledge base articles by the product feature they cover and applies consistent tags automatically, replacing ad hoc manual tagging with a standardized process that scales with the knowledge base itself.

## Outcomes

Consistent, scalable article tagging across the knowledge base — improving both content retrieval for human searchers and filterability for downstream systems like the reporting bot and duplicate-content auditor.

<LessonsLearned>
Tagging quality is a searchability problem hiding as a data-entry problem. Automating it consistently mattered more than any individual tag being perfectly chosen, because consistency is what makes a taxonomy usable for filtering at scale.
</LessonsLearned>
