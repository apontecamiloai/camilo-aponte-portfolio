## Context

By the time a churn conversation happens after a customer has already left, the useful moment to act on it has passed. The signals that a customer is at risk usually exist earlier — in support conversation patterns, unresolved issues, and account activity — but only if someone is systematically looking for them.

## Approach

The bot surfaces evidence-based, at-risk-account signals directly to the product team, built from real support and account activity patterns rather than intuition. The goal isn't to predict churn perfectly — it's to make sure retention conversations start from data that was already sitting in the support pipeline, instead of being reconstructed after the fact.

## Outcomes

A standing signal feed that gives the product team a head start on retention conversations, grounded in evidence rather than hindsight.

<LessonsLearned>
Churn signals are only useful if they arrive before the decision point they're meant to inform. The value of this bot isn't sophistication — it's timing: surfacing what was already knowable, early enough for someone to act on it.
</LessonsLearned>
