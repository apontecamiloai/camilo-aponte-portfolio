## Context

Reporting on Fin AI's resolution rate and knowledge base health was, at first, a manual weekly task: pull numbers from Intercom, cross-reference ClickUp, and write up what changed. That's a predictable, mechanical task — exactly the kind that shouldn't cost a person an afternoon every week.

## Approach

A scheduled script combines data from Intercom and ClickUp with internal pipeline metrics to generate the recurring performance report draft automatically, on a weekly cadence. The output is a draft, not a final report — someone still reviews it before it goes out — but the pull-and-assemble work that used to consume the most time now happens on its own.

## Outcomes

A standing operational report that's ready before anyone has to ask for it, freeing up the time that used to go into manually assembling the same numbers every week.

<LessonsLearned>
Recurring reports are a good early automation target precisely because they're boring and predictable — there's no judgment call in pulling last week's numbers, which makes it safe to automate fully and reserve human time for the parts of reporting that actually require interpretation.
</LessonsLearned>
